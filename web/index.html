SCORING MODEL
prox0.30
time0.20
beh0.25
type0.15
traj0.10
raw = 0.30*prox + 0.20*time + 0.25*beh + 0.15*type + 0.10*traj
score = 100 * (raw / sum(weights)) * track_confidence
proximity: exp(-0.35 * d / R), d from closest approach to estimated origin point, R the origin zone radius (floor 2.0 km). Centre 1.00, zone edge 0.37.
time: exp(-|dt| / 1.5 h), dt between closest approach and estimated origin time.
trajectory: 1.0 if course at closest approach is within 45 deg of origin-to-slick bearing, else 0.4.
AIS gap: 0.55 if a gap ≥ 30 min has a dead-reckoned segment within 5 km of the origin zone.
sparse gap: 0.33 instead, when the vessel has fewer than 6 genuine receptions.
confidence multiplier: in [0.10, 1.00] applied to final score. A vessel seen twice cannot outrank one seen six hundred times.
Ranked likelihood for investigation. Not legal proof of discharge.
